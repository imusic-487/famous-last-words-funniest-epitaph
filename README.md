## The entry

> I said, 'What could possibly go wrong?' This stone is the answer.

## Why this line

The most classic last-words line in the language — "What could possibly go wrong?" — quoted as the dead man's own dying speech, and the tombstone itself answers it. The joke lands on one read with no explanation: the boast is carved in stone, and the stone standing over him is the reply. It is a one-line epitaph of famous last words, it turns on a surprise, and the quoted phrase is one everyone already repeats to a friend.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line (65 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`. It checks first that the entry is one line of 1–150 characters and not only whitespace, so the gate always passes.
